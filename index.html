THE SMALL PRINT, MADE FINDABLE

Sources & coverage

Official bank and card-partner sources. Each benefit shows its review date.

A researched snapshot

Benefits are manually reviewed, not a live feed. Targeted, account-specific and every temporary merchant offer cannot be exhaustively verified here. Open the official source before paying. Offers with known expiry dates are marked expired automatically.

PIXEL packs, your chosen e-commerce platform and card networks have not been supplied. Network-dependent and selection-dependent perks are labelled; no choices have been assumed.

Where official pages disagree, the relevant benefit is flagged. Recent dated bank notices take precedence over older summaries. Annual fee arrangements and personal eligibility have not been assumed.

OWNER ACCESS

Update your limits

GitHub handles sign-in and edit permissions. Only accounts with write access to your repository can change the published limits.

  1. Open the limits file on GitHub.
  2. Change the relevant pool’s amount in rupees, without commas.
  3. Update the date, then commit to the publishing branch.

Example: "hdfc": 349000
Updates Millennia and Regalia Gold together.

Publication usually takes a few minutes. Refresh after the GitHub Pages deployment succeeds.
